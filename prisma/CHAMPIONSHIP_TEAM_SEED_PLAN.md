## 📋 Forms & Data Collection

### 1. Daily Wellness Check
**Fields:**
- Sleep Quality (1-5 rating)
- Sleep Hours (number)
- Soreness Level (1-5 rating)
- Energy Level (1-5 rating)
- Stress Level (1-5 rating)
- Mood (1-5 rating)
- Hydration (text: Good/Fair/Poor)
- Comments (optional text)

**Responses:** All 25 players × 28 days = 700 responses

### 2. Post-Match Recovery Form
**Fields:**
- Muscle Soreness (1-10 rating)
- Fatigue Level (1-10 rating)
- Sleep Quality Post-Match (1-5 rating)
- Hydration Status (select)
- Any Pain/Discomfort (text)
- Recovery Activities (checkboxes)

**Responses:** Match players (14-16 per match) × 4 matches = ~60 responses

### 3. Injury Report Form
**Fields:**
- Injury Type (select: Muscle, Ligament, Joint, etc.)
- Body Part (select)
- Severity (select: Minor, Moderate, Severe)
- Mechanism (text: How it happened)
- Treatment Required (checkboxes)
- Expected Return Date (date)

**Responses:** 5 injuries across the month

### 4. GPS/Load Monitoring Form
**Fields:**
- Total Distance (km)
- High-Speed Running (m)
- Sprint Distance (m)
- Accelerations (count)
- Decelerations (count)
- Player Load (AU)
- Heart Rate Avg (bpm)
- Heart Rate Max (bpm)

**Responses:** Training participants × 16 sessions = ~320 responses

## 📈 Expected Reporting Scenarios

With this data, users can create reports showing:

1. **Player Wellness Trends** - Line chart of wellness scores over 4 weeks
2. **Training Load Distribution** - Bar chart by position/player
3. **Match Performance Comparison** - Multi-player performance across matches
4. **Injury Incidence** - Pie chart of injury types
5. **Recovery Patterns** - Post-match wellness decline and recovery
6. **Acute:Chronic Load Ratios** - Identify injury risk
7. **Attendance Patterns** - Who's missing training?
8. **Dashboard Example** - Multi-chart view with KPIs (avg wellness, total injuries, training compliance)

This data structure will allow for meaningful reports that demonstrate the full capability of the reporting system!
